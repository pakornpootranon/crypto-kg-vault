# Thesis: 2027 Ethereum DeFi bull
Example thesis note shipped with the seed vault. Replace with your own view; it is a template for how to wire pillars and kill switches to narrative nodes, not a recommendation.
Standing view that a very bullish crypto market in 2027 lands disproportionately on Ethereum DeFi tokens with real fee capture, because this cycle's growth engine is tokenization and stablecoins settling on Ethereum, and because the fee-switch regime finally connects usage to tokens.

## Pillars (each maps to a narrative node)
1. [[Tokenization and RWA]]: institutional assets move on-chain; Ethereum holds two thirds of RWA value.
2. [[Fee Switch and Buybacks]]: usage now reaches holders (UNI, AAVE pending, ENA, HYPE).
3. [[Robinhood Chain Effect]]: distribution solved by incumbents building on public rails with regulator sign-off.
4. [[Stablecoin Settlement Layer]]: pie grows faster than Ethereum share shrinks.

## What the thesis needs to see
- ETH fees and active addresses keep rising from the September base ($15.9 m, 10.3 m)
- Robinhood Chain volume holds in October without the gas subsidy
- Aavenomics 3.0 buybacks switch on; Morpho activates its fee switch
- US regulatory path (CME, ETFs, CFTC) keeps widening; MiCA yield ban stays EU-only

## Kill switches
- Ethereum stablecoin share falls below 40% with absolute supply flat
- Robinhood Chain revenue to Arbitrum falls back toward $1 m/month
- A second Kelp-scale contagion event in Aave
- US regulators reverse on buybacks or tokenized-stock exemptions
- Starknet-style L1 exits become a trend among top-5 L2s
