Lending share winner (9% to 19% YTD; 17% of active loans among large lenders, doubled in a year) powering Robinhood Earn. Fees +43% in July when Robinhood Earn went live.

The vault's clearest "growth without cash flow" case: MORPHO did not re-rate alongside UNI because its fee switch is still off, so holders own the growth but not yet the revenue [21Shares - Oct 06, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/150886607272549850FFAC8301B80D00?cnum=6).
