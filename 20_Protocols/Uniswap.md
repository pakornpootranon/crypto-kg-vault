# Uniswap
The DEX that is capturing the Robinhood Chain and Base tokenized-stock flow, and the cleanest example in the vault of a fee switch that actually reaches the token.

## Fundamentals
- September protocol fees $203.2 m (+85% m/m, second best month ever); 69% from Robinhood Chain; $15.5 m reached UNI holders via burn, a record [21Shares - Oct 06, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/150886607272549850FFAC8301B80D00?cnum=6)
- Active users +42.3% in 30 days to 2.9 m (Token Terminal) [Yahoo! Finance - Oct 07, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/C8C36073A7AED7FD2B07F686162B5C8A?cnum=2)

- Burn rate stabilised near 3 to 4% annually once price tripled (Standard Chartered)
