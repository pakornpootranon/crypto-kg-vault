# Aave
Largest DeFi lender by TVL. Current story: V4 rollout, tokenized-stock collateral, Aavenomics 3.0 buybacks, and a lending market share that is slipping to Morpho and Spark.

## Fundamentals
- TVL bottomed near $11.9 b in June, back to about $18.4 b (+50%); DAO run-rate revenue fell to about $60 m/yr; trades around 43x revenue vs about 200x in 2021 [Discover Crypto - Oct 12, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/1DCB8091C3BCCE68226AA33274C6B263?cnum=4)
- V4 passed $1 b deposits in September; live on Avalanche, Arc, Base
- Lending share fell from about 59% to 41% YTD; RWA lending share 62% to 33%; daily revenue $261 k to $158 k [Anue - Oct 08, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/EAD26043C03AF5CA32D35619B5F9C537?cnum=1)
- Equities Hub on Base (Sep 25): seven US stocks as collateral to borrow USDC; Chainlink feeds stale for 48 h each weekend

## Value accrual
- Buybacks acquired 205 k AAVE (1.28% of supply, about $42 m) in ten months, then paused after the Kelp DAO exploit; Aavenomics 3.0 proposes immutable automated buybacks about $30 m/yr (about 1.2% of supply) and a possible burn
- "Aave Will Win": 100% of revenue from Aave-branded products to the DAO
- Security: $1.5 m V4 program, formal verification, AI-assisted scanning [Crypto Briefing - Oct 08, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/665B22F543F44F30652D00B8BD2B6B98?cnum=1)

## Risks
- MiCA / ECB stablecoin yield ban widening explicitly names Aave
- Collateral contagion (Kelp DAO exploit, about $300 m)
- Share loss to [[Morpho]] on the Robinhood channel
