# Optimism (OP Mainnet and OP Stack)
Two things in one node: OP Mainnet (the rollup) and Optimism the *chain operator* business, which now runs sequencing and infra for Unichain, Ink and Soneium.

## Snapshot
- Unichain transfers production sequencing, node access, monitoring and incident response to Optimism (testnet Oct 13, mainnet Oct 29) [Crypto Wire - Oct 11, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/FF36E4541F30F0685155929048C3000B?cnum=1)
- Base paid the Optimism Collective about $711 k in September [Anue - Oct 11, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/7D11EFCBA2C3437E006ED788DC842DF5?cnum=3)

## Thesis angle
OP is the "picks and shovels" claim on OP Stack chains. Single-operator dependency is also the risk: one provider failure can hit several brands at once.
