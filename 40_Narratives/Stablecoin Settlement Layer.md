# Stablecoin Settlement Layer

Ethereum holds about 47 to 48% of on-chain stablecoin supply ($146.5 b) and about 57% including L2s. Dominance drifts down while the pie grows; a $3 t stablecoin market in ten years at 30% share is still about $1 t on Ethereum. Consortium stablecoins (Global Dollar/USDG with Arbitrum, OpenUSD) compete on distribution.
