# Regulation SEC CFTC MiCA

- SEC and CFTC building a pro-crypto framework; SEC cleared token buybacks; Sep 17 exemption for Robinhood tokenized stocks (five years)
- CME listing UNI futures Oct 19; CFTC working to bring Hyperliquid to the US

## EU (headwind)
- ECB moving to widen the MiCA stablecoin yield ban; Circle, Aave and users resist
- EU anti-corruption strategy targets crypto and opaque ownership (Oct 9)
- MiFID II treatment of perps under review

Net: US-listed and US-regulated access (CME, ETFs) favours large-cap DeFi; yield products carry EU risk.
