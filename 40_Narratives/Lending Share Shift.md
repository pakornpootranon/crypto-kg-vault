# Lending Share Shift

Crypto lending grew 55% since July (Cointelegraph via Crypto Briefing, Oct 8), but share moved: Aave 59% to 41%, Morpho 9% to 19%, Spark 5% to 10% YTD. Aave's RWA-lending share fell 62% to 33%. Aave's response: new collateral types (tokenized stocks), V4 multi-chain, buybacks.
