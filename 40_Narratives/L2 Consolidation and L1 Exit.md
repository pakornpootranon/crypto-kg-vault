# L2 Consolidation and L1 Exit

Two opposite moves inside one narrative: L2s consolidating under a few operators (Optimism running Unichain, Ink, Soneium; Base and Arbitrum taking share) and a hot L2 (Starknet) threatening to leave Ethereum altogether.

## Evidence
- Ethereum Foundation (Mar 2026): L2 mission should include differentiated features, not just scaling [Anue - Oct 12, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/26E9A3E7004AA14BF8C2298C5C8157A7?cnum=1)
- L2 transactions 135.6 m (Jan 2024) to 543.8 m (Mar 2026); Base, Arbitrum, Optimism are the top three; ZK rollups lost throughput
- Smaller rollups expected to keep losing users to Arbitrum and Base
- Starknet considering L1 for post-quantum agility; market paid up 82% in a week for the idea

## Trade implication
Own the operators and the hubs ([[OP]], [[ARB]]), fade generic L2 tokens, treat L1-exit stories as event trades not theses.
