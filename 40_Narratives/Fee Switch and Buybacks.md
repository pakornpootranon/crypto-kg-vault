# Fee Switch and Buybacks

"Crypto tokens are starting to look like equity." Tokens with fees that reach holders re-rated; tokens without did not.

## Evidence
- Token buybacks hit a record $638 m after SEC clearance [Crypto Wire - Sep 26, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/6E2FE91C403BC99BD68889E1BB026148?cnum=7)
- UNI: switch on since Dec 2025, $15.5 m to holders in September, burn 3 to 4%/yr
- AAVE: 205 k bought back then paused; Aavenomics 3.0 immutable buybacks about $30 m/yr proposed
- ENA: 95% of net revenue to buyback and burn
- HYPE: fee-funded burns, $1.4 b revenue
- MORPHO: switch off, no re-rate: the control case

## The discipline
Compare net burns against new issuance and unlocks before comparing to valuation. A 5% burn against 8% emissions is dilution with good PR.

## Regulatory enabler
CFTC and SEC moving to allow direct value capture; previously the main reason switches stayed off.
