# Robinhood Chain Effect

An incumbent broker put 27 m users on an Ethereum L2, paying gas in ETH, with regulator sign-off. Beneficiaries are concrete and measurable.

## Chain of value
Robinhood users -> [[Robinhood Chain]] (Arbitrum Orbit) -> swaps on [[Uniswap]] (69% of Sep fees) -> yield on [[Morpho]] (Robinhood Earn) -> settlement on [[Ethereum]] -> sequencer revenue to [[Arbitrum]] ($1 m to $4 to 5 m/month)

## Test ahead
Gas subsidy expired end of September. October data and Robinhood's late-October Q3 earnings show whether activity holds without it [21Shares - Oct 06, 2026](https://app.bigdata.com/documents/150886607272549850FFAC8301B80D00?cnum=6). Standard Chartered expects more platforms to replicate the setup.
