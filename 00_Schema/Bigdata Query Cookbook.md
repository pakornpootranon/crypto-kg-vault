# Bigdata query cookbook
One focus, one period, one entity group per call. Natural language, no keyword bags.

- "Which DeFi tokens on Ethereum are gaining social attention and trending narratives this week"
- "Ethereum Layer 2 tokens Arbitrum Optimism Starknet Base adoption and price narrative"
- "Tokenized stocks and real world assets on Ethereum DeFi this week"
- "Stablecoin supply changes on Ethereum Arbitrum Base this week"
- "Token unlocks to watch next week"

- "Aave protocol news and AAVE token sentiment in the last month"
- "Uniswap UNI token fee switch and protocol news in the last month"
- "Ethena ENA and USDe supply and yield news in the last month"
- "Morpho lending market share and MORPHO token news"
- "Hyperliquid HYPE perpetual DEX dominance and buyback narrative"

## Research-house catalysts
- "Standard Chartered crypto research price targets DeFi tokens"

- text: "Ethena ENA token foundation agreement", context: "search in SEC filings" (found the StableCoinX 8-K)
